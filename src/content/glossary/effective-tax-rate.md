## Definition

The effective tax rate is the average rate at which your total income is taxed. It is calculated by dividing your total tax bill by your total taxable income and multiplying by 100 to get a percentage.

## Explanation

Unlike your marginal rate (the rate on your last dollar), your effective rate reflects your actual overall tax burden. Because progressive systems tax only each portion of income at its respective bracket rate, most taxpayers have an effective rate significantly lower than their top marginal rate.

## Formula

```
Effective Tax Rate = (Total Tax ÷ Total Taxable Income) × 100
```

## Examples

- Taxable income of $85,000, total tax of $14,000:
  - Effective Rate = ($14,000 ÷ $85,000) × 100 = **16.5%**
  - Marginal Rate might be 22% or 24%

## Why It Matters

- **Comparison**: The effective rate lets you compare tax burdens across different income levels or countries.
- **Planning**: Understanding your effective rate helps you evaluate the impact of tax planning strategies.
