---
term: "Hong Kong Income Tax"
category: "Income Tax"
definition: "Hong Kong income tax refers to the territorial tax system where only income sourced in Hong Kong is taxable. Main tax types include Salaries Tax (2%-17% progressive or 15% standard rate), Profits Tax (8.25%-16.5%), Property Tax (15%), and Stamp Duty. No capital gains tax, no VAT, and no sales tax."
