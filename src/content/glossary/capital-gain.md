## Definition

A capital gain is the profit realized from the sale of a capital asset — such as stocks, real estate, bonds, or other investments — that has increased in value since purchase.

## Explanation

Capital gains arise when you sell an asset for more than your **cost basis** (the original purchase price plus certain costs). Only the gain is taxed, not the total proceeds.

### Short-Term vs. Long-Term

- **Short-term capital gains** apply to assets held for one year or less and are taxed at ordinary income rates.
- **Long-term capital gains** apply to assets held for more than one year and are typically taxed at preferential rates.

## Examples

- Buy stock for $50, sell for $75 → Capital gain of $25.
- Buy a rental property for $200,000, sell for $250,000 after 3 years → Capital gain of $50,000 (long-term).
