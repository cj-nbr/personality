## Definition

Gross income is your total income from all sources before any deductions or adjustments are applied. It includes wages, salaries, bonuses, tips, investment income, rental income, and other earnings.

## Explanation

Gross income is the starting point for calculating your taxes. It encompasses all taxable income streams before any reductions.

For individuals, gross income typically includes:
- Wages, salaries, and tips
- Business income
- Investment income (dividends, interest, capital gains)
- Rental income
- Alimony received (under pre-2019 divorce agreements in the U.S.)
- Miscellaneous income

## Examples

- An employee earning $75,000 in salary from one employer has gross income of $75,000.
- A freelancer earning $50,000 from client work plus $2,000 in bank interest has gross income of $52,000.
- An investor who sells stock for a $3,000 profit and receives $500 in dividends has investment gross income of $3,500.
