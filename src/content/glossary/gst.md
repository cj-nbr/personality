## Definition

Goods and Services Tax (GST) is a value-based consumption tax applied to most goods and services sold for domestic consumption. GST is used in India, Australia, Canada, Singapore, and other countries.

## Explanation

GST is similar to VAT in concept — it is a multi-stage tax on the value added at each stage of the supply chain. Registered businesses charge GST on sales (output) and claim credit for GST paid on purchases (input), remitting the difference to the government.

## Examples

- In India, GST is applied at rates of 0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, and 28% depending on the type of goods or service.
- In Australia, GST is a flat 10% on most goods and services.
