## Definition

Net income is the amount of money you have left after all deductions, taxes, and expenses have been subtracted from your gross income. Also known as "take-home pay" for individuals or "net profit" for businesses.

## Explanation

For individuals, net income can mean two things depending on context:

1. **Personal (after tax)**: Your take-home pay — gross income minus taxes and payroll deductions.
2. **Business (after all expenses)**: Revenue minus all operating expenses, interest, and taxes, also known as the bottom line.

For tax calculation purposes, net income typically refers to taxable income after deductions and credits have been applied — the amount that determines your final tax bill or refund.

## Examples

- A salaried employee with a gross salary of $60,000 who pays $12,000 in taxes and $3,000 in pre-tax deductions has a net income (take-home pay) of $45,000.
- A business with $200,000 in revenue and $150,000 in total expenses has a net income (profit) of $50,000.
