## What Is Income Tax?

Income tax is a direct tax levied by a government on the income earned by individuals and businesses. It is one of the most important sources of revenue for governments worldwide, funding public services such as education, healthcare, infrastructure, and national defense.

## How Income Tax Works

### Taxable Income

Not all income is taxed equally. The process typically works as follows:

1. **Gross Income**: This is your total income from all sources — wages, salaries, bonuses, investments, rental income, and more.
2. **Adjustments and Deductions**: Certain amounts may be subtracted from gross income, such as contributions to retirement accounts or specific business expenses.
3. **Taxable Income**: The amount remaining after adjustments and deductions is your taxable income — the figure that your actual tax bill is based on.

### Tax Rates

Most countries use a **progressive tax system**, meaning that higher income levels are taxed at higher rates. This is different from a flat tax, where everyone pays the same rate regardless of income.

### Filing

Taxpayers typically report their income and calculate their tax liability annually. Filing can be done electronically, by paper, or through a tax professional. Many countries have set deadlines — often in the spring — by which returns must be submitted.

<InfoNote>
  Income tax rules vary significantly by country. The examples provided here are general and educational. Always refer to your local tax authority for the specific rules that apply to your situation.
</InfoNote>

## Common Misconceptions

### "I don't make enough to file taxes"

Many jurisdictions require filing even if your income is below the standard deduction threshold. Filing can also be beneficial if you're eligible for refundable credits.

### "Tax evaders just don't pay taxes"

Tax avoidance (legal minimization of tax liability) and tax evasion (illegal non-payment) are very different. Understanding the tax code to use legitimate deductions and credits is perfectly legal and encouraged.

## Key Takeaways

- Income tax is a tax on earnings, levied by governments at national and sometimes local levels.
- Progressive systems tax higher incomes at higher rates.
- Understanding your taxable income and the deductions and credits available to you can significantly reduce your tax bill.
- When in doubt, consult a qualified tax professional for personalized advice.
