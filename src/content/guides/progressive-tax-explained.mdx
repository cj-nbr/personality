## What Is a Progressive Tax?

A progressive tax system is one where the tax rate increases as the taxable income increases. In other words, people with higher incomes pay a larger percentage of their income in taxes than those with lower incomes.

## How Progressive Taxation Works

### Tax Brackets

In a progressive system, income is divided into **brackets**. Each bracket is taxed at a different rate:

- **Lower brackets** — taxed at lower rates
- **Higher brackets** — taxed at higher rates

Only the income within each bracket is taxed at that bracket's rate. This is a common point of confusion — moving into a higher bracket does not mean all of your income is taxed at the higher rate.

<Example title="How Brackets Work in Practice">
Imagine a simplified tax system with three brackets:

| Taxable Income | Rate |
|---|---|
| $0 – $40,000 | 10% |
| $40,001 – $80,000 | 20% |
| Above $80,000 | 30% |

If you earn $90,000, your tax is calculated as:
- First $40,000 × 10% = $4,000
- Next $40,000 × 20% = $8,000
- Remaining $10,000 × 30% = $3,000
- **Total tax = $15,000**

Your effective tax rate is 16.7%, not 30%.
</Example>

## Why Progressive Taxation?

### Vertical Equity

Progressive taxation is based on the principle of **vertical equity** — those with a greater ability to pay should contribute more. This helps reduce income inequality and funds public goods that benefit society as a whole.

### Revenue Stability

Progressive systems generate stable revenue because tax collections grow with the economy. During economic booms, higher incomes push taxpayers into higher brackets; during downturns, lower incomes reduce collections somewhat, providing a natural stabilizer.

## Progressive vs. Flat vs. Regressive

| System | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Progressive** | Rate increases with income | Most U.S. federal income tax |
| **Flat** | Same rate for all incomes | Some state income taxes |
| **Regressive** | Rate decreases as income rises | Sales tax (takes larger share of low-income spending) |

## Common Questions

### Does moving to a higher bracket cost me money?

No. Only the income above the bracket threshold is taxed at the higher rate. Your total tax bill always increases more slowly than your income.

### Is progressive taxation fair?

Fairness in taxation is a value judgment. Progressive systems aim to distribute the tax burden more equitably based on ability to pay. Arguments for and exist in economic and political discourse.

## Summary

Progressive taxation ensures that tax rates rise with income, promoting fairness and funding essential public services. Understanding brackets and rates helps you plan your finances more effectively.
