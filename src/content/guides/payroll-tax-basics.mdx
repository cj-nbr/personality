facts:
  - The U.S. Social Security tax rate is 12.4%, split evenly between employer and employee at 6.2% each.
  - Medicare tax has no wage base limit, unlike Social Security which has an annual cap.
  - Self-employed individuals pay both the employer and employee portions through the Self-Employment Tax.
  - Some states in the U.S. impose additional payroll taxes for disability insurance and paid family leave.
