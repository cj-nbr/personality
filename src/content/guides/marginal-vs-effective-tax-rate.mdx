  - Your marginal tax rate determines how much you save on each additional dollar of deductions.
  - The effective tax rate for U.S. households varies significantly, with the top 1% often paying a lower effective rate than the middle class due to capital gains treatment.
  - A $1,000 tax deduction saves $220 for someone in the 22% bracket, but only $120 for someone in the 12% bracket.
