  - Bracket creep occurs when inflation pushes taxpayers into higher brackets without an actual increase in real income
  - Your effective tax rate is almost always lower than your marginal tax rate
  - The U.S. has had seven federal income tax brackets for many years, though the income thresholds change annually
  - Some countries use flat tax systems with only one bracket instead of progressive brackets
quiz:
  - question: What is bracket creep?
    options:
      - When your tax bracket mysteriously disappears
      - When inflation pushes you into a higher bracket without real income growth
      - When you forget to file taxes
      - When deductions increase your bracket
  - question: What is the difference between marginal and effective tax rates?
    options:
      - They are the same
      - Marginal is the rate on your last dollar; effective is your total tax divided by total income
      - Marginal is always lower than effective
      - Effective only applies to corporations
  - question: What can help you drop into a lower tax bracket?
    options:
      - Earning more income
      - Increasing deductions to reduce taxable income
      - Filing later
      - Ignoring the rules
